# TikTok Studio upload file

Use the `*-1080p-upload.mp4` file for PC TikTok Studio. The separate `*-4k-master.mp4` is an archival upscale and is not the recommended upload for these source recordings.

## What is in the upload MP4

- MP4 container, H.264 video, 1080 × 1920 portrait (9:16), constant 60 fps, 4:2:0 pixel format, Rec.709 SDR.
- AAC stereo, 48 kHz. The Fish Oil v4 mix measures −14.61 LUFS integrated / −1.79 dBTP; Ashwagandha v4 measures −14.71 LUFS / −1.75 dBTP.
- The source videos are 1080 × 1920 and 60 fps. The 4K archives are rendered at 2160 × 3840 from that footage and Remotion graphics; they do not add captured detail.
- The exports are MP4s with a fast-start layout. Their final decode, dimensions, frame rate, audio stream and duration are checked by the render scripts.

TikTok Studio’s web upload guidance currently lists MP4 or WebM, at least 720 × 1280, less than 10 GB and no more than 30 minutes. These two files are 1080 × 1920, about 27 seconds and 48 seconds respectively, comfortably under those listed limits. See [TikTok Studio creator tools](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-tools-on-tiktok).

## In TikTok Studio on PC

1. Upload the `1080p-upload.mp4` file; keep the 4K file as the local archive.
2. Select the included frame-zero cover image in TikTok Studio instead of relying on automatic cover selection. The cover is a real frame with editorial typography, not a generated photo.
3. Review the full uploaded preview and listen for speech/SFX, caption placement, product link, affiliate disclosure and CTA. Check the rendered preview again after any Studio edit.
4. Keep the original MP4. TikTok can transcode uploaded video for delivery, and no export setting prevents platform recompression or guarantees that every viewer receives the same resolution/bitrate.

The purpose of the 1080p file is to give Studio a clean vertical master that matches the source footage and avoids uploading an unnecessary 4K upscale. It cannot force a platform bitrate or remove recompression.
