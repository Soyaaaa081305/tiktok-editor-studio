## Research used for added text

Magnesium contributes to normal nerve and muscle function. Supplement labels distinguish elemental magnesium from the weight of a magnesium compound; the editor must not infer elemental quantity from compound mass. [NIH Office of Dietary Supplements: Magnesium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/)

Some small studies suggest possible stress or sleep benefits from certain ashwagandha preparations; products, methods and doses vary, and results are not proof for this bottle. The upper graphic says “May help; research is limited,” with a separate readable “Results vary” passage. [NIH Office of Dietary Supplements: Ashwagandha](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Ashwagandha-HealthProfessional/)

The video makes no guaranteed magnesium-related sleep claim. Research on magnesium for insomnia remains limited and conflicting. [NCCIH: Sleep Disorders and Complementary Health Approaches](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-and-complementary-health-approaches)

“This really helped me, sa totoo lang” is retained as the creator's recorded personal experience, with an explicit personal-experience label and “Individual results vary.” It is not independent product proof.
