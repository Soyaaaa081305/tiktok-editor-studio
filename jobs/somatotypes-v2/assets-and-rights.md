# Somatotypes · assets and rights

The original source files are in ignored `public/somatotypes/` for this render only. They are not committed or uploaded to GitHub because several show private family, school or vaccination contexts. The finished MP4 itself is the authorized deliverable.

The additional 44 kg family-walk and back photos, 76 kg clip `76-b.mp4`, school/uniform image, and vaccination images were supplied but are not used in this cut.

School-uniform and vaccination-card images are excluded from the edit. No raw inputs, extracted stills, ASR caches, intermediate renders or personal photos are included in GitHub.

## Audio

- Music is an original deterministic synth/beat generated by `build-audio.mjs`, because the creator previously requested a suspense riser and hip-hop/electronic drop at 0:08. It is an explicit per-job exception to the no-music default.
- Selected SFX are from the project's locally packaged `public/sfx` library and are listed with cue frames in `edit-data.json`; no internet music or unlicensed third-party track is added.

## Deliverables

The private GitHub release contains the final 1080p upload MP4, 4K upscale master, frame-0 cover and this job's editable source/documentation bundle. It excludes the creator's original videos and personal stills. Their hashes may be kept in the local-only render audit; do not place them in the repository/release manifest.
