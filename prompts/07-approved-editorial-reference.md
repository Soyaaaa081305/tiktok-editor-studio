# Approved editorial reference · Noda.lifts

Version 1.0 · 6 October 2026

Use this as the production reference for future Noda.lifts recordings. The creator approved the Ashwagandha gapless motion-graphics edit as the style he wants to keep consistent. The ATC Fish Oil v4 edit is being brought into this same visual system.

## Keep consistent

- Open with a real frame from the creator's or product's footage. Keep the cover still for 6 frames at 60 fps (0.1 s), then dissolve quickly into the moving hook.
- Make the first ten seconds the most considered part: establish a clear question or tension immediately, pay it off early, use a purposeful crop/motion change, and introduce the first useful visual explanation before the hook goes flat.
- Use clean, human-looking footage and editorial typography. Prefer natural color, restrained contrast, clear type, and one simple accent. Keep the creator and actual product authentic. Do not generate synthetic product photography, fake testimonials, generic molecule art, fake muscle/body illustrations, or decorative AI-looking backgrounds.
- Alternate direct-to-camera speech, a stacked upper visual with the same presenter continuing below, and full-screen real footage or bespoke diagrams. Choose the mode from the words. An upper insert or full-screen visual never removes or delays the recorded speech.
- Use bespoke motion graphics to clarify a mechanism, comparison, number, or evidence boundary. Build, settle, and exit each graphic on the spoken idea. Make complex visuals useful, not busy; use plain footage for simple beats.
- Keep captions light, phrase-based, readable on mobile, and placed to avoid faces, product labels, and TikTok interface areas. Emphasize only meaningful terms. Do not add a fixed heavy box or bounce each word by default.
- Use selective audible sound effects on meaningful reveals and transitions. No added music unless the creator asks for it. Keep speech clear and measure the final mix.
- Let the middle breathe while staying tied to the speech. Tighten the product, takeaway, CTA, and creator signature at the end. On Noda.lifts, scripts include the spoken line: “Like and follow for more science-based lifting advice. God bless!” If an already-recorded video lacks that pickup, use a restrained visible overlay on the active final shot; never invent a voice.

## Prevent the previous pause defect

Treat the edited speech EDL as the single timing source. Build the voice track first, then place captions, graphics, transitions, and SFX over that continuous track. A B-roll insert must never become a silent hold. For every full-screen scene, record the exact spoken source/output range that supports it. Keep authored silence to the 0.1-second cover only unless a source pause is both intentional and explicitly documented. Run a voice-only silence scan before picture export, inspect every flag, then check caption start/end timing against the rendered dialogue.

Do not show an unexplained title or CTA after its related line ends. If the source has a pause, trim or reconnect only when the sentence remains natural and complete. Do not fill a pause with a synthetic voice or a graphic-only hold.

## Platform-ready output

- Deliver a high-quality 1080 × 1920, 60 fps, H.264/AAC upload file when the source supports 60 fps. Keep the 2160 × 3840 4K upscale as an archive master only; upscaling cannot restore detail absent from the source.
- TikTok may re-encode uploaded files. No editor setting can promise uncompressed playback. TikTok Studio for web currently documents MP4/WebM, at least 720 × 1280, under 10 GB, and no more than 30 minutes; 1080 × 1920 is the practical upload target here.
- Provide the frame-0 cover as a separate image so the creator can select it in TikTok Studio. The 0.1-second frame can help cover capture but does not guarantee the platform will select it automatically.

## Quality contract

Use the strongest result the source and available evidence support. Review story, speech continuity, science claims, actual footage, motion, captions, sound, export metadata, and cover. Fix remediable issues. Report unverified subjective listening or device/platform checks instead of treating a score as a guarantee. Consistent style means repeatable craft decisions, not identical graphics on every topic.
