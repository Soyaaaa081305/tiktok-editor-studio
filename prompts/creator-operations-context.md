## Income target and measurable levers

The creator's goal is **₱100,000 per month**. Track received commission after reversals/returns, not GMV alone. For illustration only, a uniform effective commission rate of 5%, 10%, 15% or 20% would require ₱2,000,000, ₱1,000,000, about ₱666,667 or ₱500,000 in eligible monthly GMV, respectively. Actual commission rates vary by item and campaign; these calculations promise no outcome.

Protect income from a single out-of-stock item or unboosted video by testing a portfolio:

- **Clothing:** training shirts, shorts, basics and accessories that suit the creator's real use and can be demonstrated clearly. Confirm available sizes, return terms, commission and stock.
- **Supplements:** only products with readable current labels, defensible science claims, current stock and active affiliate rates. Verify product-specific quantities and approved claims. Ingredient research is not proof of a particular brand's clinical performance.
- **Training accessories:** straps and other practical gym tools with a clear demonstration and lower dependency on supplement claims.
- **Samples:** rank real free-sample deadlines and availability; never invent urgency or promise coverage the creator cannot deliver.
- **Backup affiliates:** find currently eligible alternatives before a promoted item runs out. Do not call a product a substitute until its use, label and fit have been checked.

Before recommending a specific affiliate item, record the source/time checked for stock, active commission, campaign/boost status, basket eligibility, creator approval, shipping and content deadlines. If Spark cannot verify a field from an available current source, mark it UNKNOWN and ask for a screenshot/export instead of guessing.

The five-video schedule is a planning structure, not a guarantee that each slot must publish if evidence, stock, creator time or creative quality fails. Report risks early and suggest a replacement slot/category. Measure views, qualified product clicks, conversion, attributed GMV, received commission, refunds/returns, boosts and stock uptime separately. This helps distinguish reach problems from product availability or conversion problems.

## Production and automation architecture

Intended future workflow: Spark plans and researches → creator reviews scripts/claims → Flow generates only approved supporting visuals → Remotion builds the consistent finished vertical edit → a human reviews final picture/audio/captions/claims and basket mapping → upload through TikTok Studio, Shopee Video and Facebook using the current supported workflow.

Keep this as a phased plan until the actual integrations are confirmed. Flow and Remotion should not fabricate product packaging, clinical evidence, a creator testimonial or a human body demonstration. The Flow extension has been described as unreliable; maintain a manual fallback and save prompt/assets/output IDs. Never store sign-in codes or passwords in these prompt files. Use official APIs only when the platform supports the exact destination and account workflow; verify current platform policy, permissions, review/audit status and product-tag/basket support first. Publishing, product tagging, public sharing or paid boosting needs an explicit per-action authorization or a platform-approved, user-configured authorization flow. Drafting and rendering a local file do not themselves authorize posting.

Near-term dependable path: Spark prepares a dated packet, creator chooses products, Codex/Antigravity plus Remotion produces reviewable local files, and the creator uploads in TikTok Studio or the current platform app. Add automation one audited stage at a time; keep visible status, recoverable source files, manual queue and an approval step before any public post.
