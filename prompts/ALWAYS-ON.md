# ALWAYS-ON PRODUCTION DIRECTIVES

Persistent creator and editing rules for @noda.lifts TikTok production. Apply this file before every script generation, editing task, or render review. Direct user instructions supersede defaults.

---

## 1. Core Visual Architecture (News Daddy 55 / 45 Split)

Every production video strictly uses the **News Daddy (Dylan Page)** two-layer vertical split:
- **Canvas Resolution:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical, 60 fps or source-matched).
- **Top 55% (Y: 0 to 1056px):**
  - Dedicated Proof, B-Roll, & Graphics zone.
  - Content: Peer-reviewed study extracts, PubMed/meta-analysis charts, product macro footage, anatomical motion diagrams, meme clips, or split comparisons ("Mali To" red vs. "Ganito Dapat" green).
  - Direct cuts synchronized to spoken nouns and claim shifts.
- **Divider (Y: 1051 to 1061px):**
  - **10px solid gold bar (`#FFE600`)** with subtle drop shadow separating top and bottom.
- **Bottom 45% (Y: 1056 to 1920px):**
  - Dedicated Presenter Talking Head zone.
  - Framing: Tight chest-up framing, centered, direct eye contact with the lens.
  - Uninterrupted presenter presence while the top 55% shifts with the narrative.

---

## 2. Style & Pacing (Smartfit Taglish & Zero Dead-Air)

- **Language & Tone:**
  - Conversational **Taglish** matching Enver Florendo (Smartfit) cadence.
  - Natural code-switching ("Wag kang magpauto sa mga mamahaling creatine...", "Ayon sa recent meta-analyses...", "Sobrang laki ng difference...").
  - Direct, honest, science-based authority without condescension.
- **Pacing & Cutting:**
  - **Zero Dead-Air:** Aggressively cut pauses greater than **0.2s**.
  - Speech is the timeline master truth. Seamless jump cuts or top-half B-roll changes mask cut points.
  - High retention, rapid momentum, immediate hook with zero introductory filler.
  - Runtime: 45–60 seconds target.

---

## 3. Subtitles & Typography (Karaoke Gold Springs)

- **Positioning:**
  - **Center Y: 1380px** (precisely in the lower presenter zone, safe from TikTok UI icons, search bar, and bottom description overlay).
- **Style:**
  - Font: Bold / Black sans-serif (`Inter`, `Arial Black`, or `Montserrat`), uppercase or sentence case with high visual punch.
  - Font Size: 48px – 56px.
  - Base text: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with strong dark stroke/shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`).
- **Karaoke Word Springs:**
  - Active word highlighted in **Gold (`#FFE600`)**.
  - Animated with a snappy spring scale bounce (`1.0 -> 1.15 -> 1.0`) on entry.
  - Phrasing: 3–6 words per caption card, timed tightly to syllable/word audio cues.

---

## 4. Audio Engineering Master Rules

- **Voice Track:**
  - Original dialogue recorded with clear presence.
  - Loudness normalization: **-14 to -16 LUFS** integrated, **-1.5 dBTP** true peak ceiling.
  - Vocal clarity is paramount; do not over-compress or clip quiet consonants.
- **Music:**
  - **NO ADDED BACKGROUND MUSIC by default.**
  - Zero background music tracks unless explicitly requested by the creator.
- **Sound Effects (SFX):**
  - Meaningful, audible, and surgical.
  - Whooshes on top-split asset changes, UI pops on data callouts, subtle dings on truth reveals.
  - Mixed subordinate to speech (-12 dB to -18 dB relative to dialogue).

---

## 5. Signature & Conversion Rules

- **Noda.lifts Outro Signature:**
  - "Like and follow for more science-based lifting advice. God bless!"
  - Spoken live during active presenter delivery in the final 3–4 seconds.
  - Never place as a silent, static end card.
- **Affiliate Yellow Basket Callout:**
  - For affiliate slots (Slots 01, 03, 05): Point down or verbally cue "Check out the yellow basket sa baba."
